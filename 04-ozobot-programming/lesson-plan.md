# Ozobot Programming (Part 2) - Lesson Plan

## Overview
3-day progression from basic programming to storytelling to competition
**Classes:** STEM (51 min Wed/Thu, 33 min Fri)
**Materials:** 18 Ozobots with Bluetooth transmitters, padding for tournaments

## What You Need to Print/Prep

### Day 1 - Print shape practice sheets (16 copies)
Simple geometric shapes for students to program their Ozobots to trace:
- Square (with measurements)
- Triangle 
- Rectangle
- Plus sign (+)
Students learn movement AND can add lights/sounds for fun

### Day 2 - Print storytelling sheets (16 copies)
```
OZOBOT STORYTELLING CHALLENGE

Draw and label in each circle:
○ Circle 1: Something HAPPY
○ Circle 2: Something SAD  
○ Circle 3: Something SURPRISING
○ Circle 4: Something FUNNY

Program your Ozobot to:
1. Move to Circle 1 → play HAPPY sound
2. Move to Circle 2 → play SAD sound
3. Move to Circle 3 → play SURPRISED sound
4. Move to Circle 4 → play LAUGH sound

Tell your story!
```

### Day 3 - Bring padding/towels for table edges

## Daily Plans

### DAY 1 (Wednesday) - "Shape Programming + Effects"
**Goal:** Learn programming basics + discover lights/sounds

**5 mins:** Demo OzoBlockly connection and basic movement
**10 mins:** Students practice basic movements on their shape sheets
**20 mins:** Challenge - program exact shape tracing with measurements
**10 mins:** Exploration time - add lights, sounds, speed changes to their shapes
**6 mins:** Show off - who made the coolest light/sound effects?

**Your Role:** Help with Bluetooth, encourage experimentation with effects

### DAY 2 (Thursday) - "Storytelling Challenge"  
**Goal:** Combine movement + sounds + creativity

**5 mins:** Hand out sheets, explain storytelling concept
**15 mins:** Students draw their 4 emotion scenarios
**25 mins:** Program the movement sequence and sounds
**6 mins:** Teams share their stories (just tell the story, don't demo all bots)

**Your Role:** Help with sound programming, encourage creative stories

### DAY 3 (Friday) - "Edge of Danger Tournament" 
**Goal:** Competition using skills from previous days
**Time Crunch:** Only 33 minutes total

**3 mins:** Quick setup - move tables, lay down padding
**15 mins:** ONE qualifying attempt per team (measure and rank)
**10 mins:** Championship round - top 4 teams only
**5 mins:** Awards + cleanup

**Prizes:**
- Champion certificate (you make)
- Runner-up gets extra credit points
- Best teamwork recognition
- Everyone gets participation sticker

## Shape Sheet Design Ideas
Create simple line drawings with measurements:
- Square: 4 inches per side
- Triangle: 3 equal sides
- Rectangle: 5 inches × 3 inches
- Plus sign: intersecting lines

## Success Indicators
- Day 1: Students can program shapes AND discover lights/sounds are fun
- Day 2: Students create coherent stories with programmed sequences  
- Day 3: Tournament runs smoothly with minimal chaos

## Backup Plans
- If Bluetooth fails: Use color codes on paper instead of programming
- If tournament setup takes too long: Do "edge challenge" at regular desks with books
- If kids finish early: Let them create their own shapes or design light shows
