Canva First-Time Setup (Students)

Use your school email. Ask for help if you’re stuck.

Steps

  1. Go to canva.com
  2. Click Log in → choose Continue with Google and use your school email
  3. If it asks, pick Education and Student
  4. Click Create a design → choose Presentation (16:9)
  5. Pick a simple template (big text, clean)
  6. Rename the project at the top: Period-Last1&Last2-Career
    Example: 3-Weston&Miller-DataScientist
  7. Make your Title slide first
  8. Add slide titles only (details later)
  9. Copy the Share link so you can turn it in

Common Issues

  • Can’t log in: Make sure you used your school Google account
  • Too many choices: Choose Presentation (16:9) and a simple template
  • Forgot to save: Canva auto-saves, but still copy your Share link
Tip: Big text, few words, clear pictures. Keep it simple!
