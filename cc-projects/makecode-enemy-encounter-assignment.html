MakeCode Arcade Enemy Encounter Challenge

Creative Coding

Assignment Overview

Time to make a game! You will make a playable MakeCode Arcade game that explores design, control, and enemy (or friend) encounters. Use the mechanics we have practiced. You don't need to make a specific type of game. There are a few ground rules, but beyond that, your goal is to explore the process of video game making, and make a game that has a beginning and an end.

What You Need To Show

  • A fully working game loop the player can start, play, and win (or lose).
  • Enemy (or friend) encounters that react to the hero. This can mean projectiles, or conversation.
  • Named characters and a story that explains why the encounter matters.
  • A project title and cover art

Must-Have Mechanics

All projects—solo or team—must demonstrate these before class ends.

  • Splash screen (add last so it does not interrupt testing).
  • Hero spawns on the map and moves with the controller.
  • Solid walls.
  • At least one enemy (or friend) that spawns where intended.
  • Enemy interaction is meaningful (dialog, teleport, tile change, lose a life, etc.) and tied to your chosen mechanic.
  • Some kind of goal that must be achieved to win. Possibly ways to lose as well

You can work solo, or in teams of two. Teams of two must make a two-player game.

Game Style Options

In addition to the required mechanics, choose a style to give your game direction and personality:

Treasure Unlock

Dialog reveals a door, treasure chest, or new path.

Challenge Path

Maze or timed movement where accuracy matters.

Score or Power

Touching enemies or items changes a score, hearts, or energy meter.

Food Delivery

Enemy requests food; hero finds and returns it for a reward.

Your Own Idea

If you have an idea for a game you'd like to create, talk to me and we'll figure it out.

Finished Early?

Keep building. Add another enemy encounter, design a new level, layer in a second game style, or polish the story. Use every minute to refine your work or enhance your skills.

Sound and Headphones

Sound design is optional but encouraged. If you test music or sound effects, wear headphones so the room stays focused.

Success Scale (Proficiency 0–4)

Score What it looks like
4 Every must-have works, the chosen game style works, the game includes a clear goal or win/lose state, and the project is saved/shared correctly.
3 Most must-haves work but one glitch remains or the game style is missing/broken or the goal is confusing.
2 Core systems incomplete—enemy missing, interaction fails, or walls do not block.
1 Sprites exist but no functional interaction yet.
0 Nothing submitted.

Submission Checklist

  • Project meets every must-have mechanic.
  • At least one game style option is working.
  • Game has a goal (win, lose, finish) that can be demonstrated.
  • Project includes named characters, game title, and custom art.
  • Project file or share link is uploaded to Canvas.
